Do you want to make an impact in your community?

Join Heroes Around Me (HAM) and help us celebrate the everyday heroes who inspire change. Whether you're passionate about writing, designing posters, managing social media, researching stories, or simply brimming with creative ideas, there's a place for you in our team. Together, we can share the stories that bridge cultures, promote empathy, and inspire action.

What do you need to do?

Write

Write interview report article after every event and HAM Newsletters!

Dept. Heads

Manage Events / Marketing Depts. Have manage strategies & leadership skills.

Video Edit

Edit interview videos, trailers and social media short clips. Use edit apps skillfully.

Graphic Design

Design event posters, social media posts & website posters.

Social Media Manage

Design social media advertise strategy. Continue post organized contents.

Outreach

Continue browsing the internet to reach out to potential interviewees.

Club Leader

Start a club at your school. Conduct interview with local heroes.

Website Manage

Manage the content updates on the website. Coding ability (Java) is recommended.

Interview Moderate

Lead the entire interview, from kick off to wrap up. Should be confident & fluent.
